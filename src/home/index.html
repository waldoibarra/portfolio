The Workshop

Make it useful.
Then make it clearer.

I build software and the working systems around AI agents. I like when a good idea becomes something people can actually use, understand, and improve.

In this universe Approach Harness Review Sharing Working notes: policy, evidence, design, delivery Related: evidence and coherence →

Build it so someone else can change it.

What lives here

I like working from a clear problem toward something a team can inspect and change. That includes architecture, developer tools, practical AI, and explanations that survive handoff.

My public work is on GitHub . For career context, visit LinkedIn .

How I like to work +

I ask what problem we are solving and who will use the result. Then I name assumptions and test a small version. Feedback may change the plan. I want the next person to understand the decision without needing me in the room. See how this site applies that choice.

What is an AI harness? +

It is the instructions, tools, and checks that help people work with agents. Instructions explain the task. Tools define the agent's reach. Clear boundaries show when a person must decide. Tests and review make the resulting work easier to verify. A good harness also makes failures understandable and recovery possible.

Review and recovery +

A test does not replace judgment. I want a reviewer to see the behavior, the boundary, and what remains uncertain.

Small changes make feedback easier to use. A clear recovery path matters when an assumption fails. Inspect the evidence ladder.

Compare this with checking whether beliefs match actions .

Tools others can inspect +

Pi Lens makes tool runs expandable without changing their execution. My dotfiles expose another bounded example of agent guidance.

Sharing a working structure invites questions and improvements, not blind reuse. Explore the policy delivery note.

A piece you can inspect

Pi Lens folds Pi's tool runs into a transcript you can expand when you need details. It changes the display, not tool execution or model context. The code and limitations are public if you want to inspect them.

My dotfiles show how I configure tools and guidance around AI coding agents. I want to help teams improve their own harnesses and learn together from real work. I also like helping leaders make technical decisions with evidence.

The Observatory

Look closer.
Look again.

I am curious about why we believe what we believe. Philosophy, different worldviews, and honest conversations help me examine the picture I think I see.

Look closer Conversation Coherence Worldviews Working notes: evidence and learning map Related: decisions in the trading journal →

A good question leaves room for an answer I did not expect.

Questions worth keeping open

I enjoy Stoic inquiry, theories about reality, and geopolitics. I ask what supports an explanation and what might change my mind. Listening to someone else's view helps me find blind spots.

Thinking out loud +

I learn by discussing an idea with a person or an LLM. I also structure notes as though I were teaching someone else. My AI learning map follows a need through design and feedback. If I cannot explain a claim clearly, I look for the gap before defending it. A useful conversation makes assumptions visible without requiring agreement.

Coherence in practice +

Keeping my word matters to me. When my decisions and actions diverge, I want feedback early enough to change course. A coherent worldview should survive honest questions.

That same feedback loop appears in structured trading notes and software review .

Competing worldviews +

Stoic ideas and questions about reality interest me. An appealing explanation is not automatically evidence.

I can compare what a view explains, where it breaks, and what remains unknown. Fiction can open a question without becoming a claim about the world. Read the evidence note.

Stories at the Crossroads offer another way to explore possibilities.

Keep the question open

What supports an explanation? What would challenge it?

When a decision and its outcome disagree, that difference is information. Reviewing software and keeping a trading journal give that question different forms.

Perspective is easier to practice when I slow down. Walk into the Garden →

The Garden

Be here.
There is time.

Ambition means more to me when it leaves room for love, health, and being alive today. I practice yoga and meditation; I am still learning to pay attention.

What I return to Presence Care Time and freedom Working notes: prototypes and attention Related: useful automation →

Attention is a practice, not an achievement.

A different measure

I would rather explore among trees than buildings. A walk, a conscious breath, and time with people I love remind me why I build.

Small practices +

Yoga and meditation help me notice what is happening before I try to change it. Sometimes I simply pause and breathe consciously. I return to these practices because attention takes practice. Read why I make room for it.

Why balance belongs here +

I want more time, freedom, and knowledge for the people I care about. Health and relationships matter alongside those goals. Automation is useful when it gives us room for life outside the screen. See how private prototypes test that aim.

The Workshop explores the systems that can create that room.

Love and care +

Care belongs in how I spend time and in how I treat people. Helping someone who asks matters more than appearing to have every answer.

That changes how I listen during a conversation .

What is the work for?

More time to learn, more freedom to choose, and more care for the people I love.

These are intentions, not a scorecard. Useful automation matters when it makes room for them.

Trading as practice +

For me, trading is a psychological exercise in self-control, not a promise of results.

My public Trading Journaling Guide covers daily planning and per-trade notes. Structured tags connect setups, conditions, and behavior.

Reflection compares intention with what happened. Its weekly and monthly sections remain unfinished. Read the decision note.

That structure connects to coherence and reviewable systems .

What I am learning +

Right now I am curious about AI, marketing, and sales. I want to understand how people find useful ideas and decide which ones deserve attention.

I want to understand the requirements behind an offer, its tradeoffs, and how feedback improves it. Explaining an idea clearly is part of that inquiry.

Places and possible worlds +

I am drawn to places with more trees than buildings. Travel invites new perspectives without needing a checklist of destinations.

Time-travel fiction, especially Steins;Gate, lets me consider choices and alternative outcomes. These are imaginative questions, not claims about literal parallel realities. Read the perspectives note.

A journal as a feedback loop

The public guide describes a repeatable process, not a record of returns.

  1. Before: make a daily pre-session plan.
  2. During: write per-trade notes and tag setup, conditions, and behavior.
  3. After: compare intent with behavior and reflect.
