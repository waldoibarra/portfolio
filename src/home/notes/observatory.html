Working note: when explanations sound alike +

Metaphysics, mysticism, and Stoic questions interest me. Similar words in different theories can invite comparison without establishing agreement.

I ask what each explanation predicts or clarifies, and where their meanings diverge. I keep a question open when I cannot tell what evidence would distinguish the views. A compelling story can change which question I ask; it cannot establish an empirical answer.

This approach can leave uncertainty unresolved rather than offer a neat conclusion. That is preferable to mistaking a shared metaphor for proof. Return to worldviews or explore fiction as possibility.

Working note: map a question across a system +

When I study AI engineering, I organize knowledge around how a need becomes an outcome. My private AI Architecture Big Picture repository maps those connections. It is learning material, not a shipped AI product.

I trace requirements into architecture and data boundaries. Evaluation asks which risks a check addresses. Operations show how actual use can change the design. The map distinguishes a development agent harness from AI inside a delivered product.

Some topics have detailed explanations; others remain outlines. A neat index alone does not prove I can build or operate every system it describes. Return to learning aloud or inspect verification levels.
