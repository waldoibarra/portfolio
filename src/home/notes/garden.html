Working note: prototypes before a family app +

Software should follow a demonstrated need. It should not turn every shared intention into a metric. Private family experiments help me ask which observations matter. They do not impose a system.

My private Habit Tracker prototype tests a short routine with a review point. Another prototype turns older intentions into choices that start unverified. Neither proves a sustained benefit, completed goal, or shared commitment. No family habit application has shipped from these experiments.

My private Money Tracker prototype analyzes spending history while preserving original labels. It suggests categories only for gaps. Conflicting suggestions remain unknown, with source provenance available for review. It is a local report, not a budget or complete financial picture. It is not a production money application.

These boundaries protect time, agency, and privacy while leaving room to learn. Return to time and freedom or see a public implementation.

Working note: ambition needs room +

Yoga, meditation, and a conscious breath are ways I practice noticing before acting. They are not claims that I am always calm or that a technique guarantees health.

I prefer exploring among trees to spending every free hour in front of a screen. That preference helps me question an automation that saves clicks but costs attention or upkeep. More output is not automatically more time with the people I love.

I return to this question rather than pretending to have solved balance. Return to presence or see why a prototype can be enough.
