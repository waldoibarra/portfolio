# One person, many paths

The homepage begins with Waldo's curiosity, then opens places for building, inquiry, presence, and exploration.
Visitors choose their own direction. Every place has a plain-language label and a return to the library.
The site welcomes personal conversations as readily as professional ones.

The Workshop covers software and practical AI. The Observatory holds questions and worldviews.
The Garden makes room for presence and care. The Crossroads connects newer interests and experiments.
Love and balance connect the views rather than becoming separate attractions.
The multiverse is a brand metaphor: one Waldo, many connected perspectives.
Across software, inquiry, presence, and trading, he structures knowledge and learns from feedback.
Cross-links show specific relationships instead of repeating a slogan in every place.

LinkedIn explains professional collaboration.
GitHub's pinned work supplies project evidence; its README invites fellow builders.
Instagram offers another personal window. Contact is reachable directly from the library and every view.
Each channel offers a different depth of the same person.

Public copy draws on supplied biographies and current personal context.
It distinguishes established work from current interests, including marketing and sales.
Private images, health and intimate details, confidential work, and unsupported claims stay unpublished.
The résumé remains available at `/resume` without a homepage link pending separate content review.
