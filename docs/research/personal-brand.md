# A useful, memorable introduction

**Position Waldo through useful systems, generous knowledge sharing, and disciplined curiosity.**
The website should support a conversation, not force every visitor into a sales process.
These recommendations combine repository evidence, supplied biographies, and the sources below.
They are design judgments, not demonstrated conversion improvements.

## Research findings

| Principle | Source | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Differentiate through audience, value, narrative, and imagery | [Personal-branding literature review](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02238) | Connect professional capability with recognizable personal interests |
| Make contact straightforward | [NN/G contact-page research](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/contact-us-pages/) | Place direct contact routes near the introduction and closing |
| Support credibility through expertise, contact, and verifiable information | [Stanford credibility guidelines](https://credibility.stanford.edu/guidelines/index.html) | Link inspectable work; avoid unsupported numerical claims |
| Prioritize first-hand value, authorship, and focus | [Google helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) | Explain actual work instead of repeating broad AI promises |
| Preserve readable contrast | [WCAG contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) | Check text against production colors |
| Give controls sufficient target area | [WCAG target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) | Make navigation and contact comfortable on mobile |
| Avoid unnecessary interaction motion | [WCAG motion guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) | Prefer native disclosures and honor reduced-motion preferences |

Stanford and NN/G provide foundational research, not current portfolio conversion benchmarks.
Accessibility requirements do not mandate generic visual design.

## Avoiding generic AI-generated presentation

A [writing experiment](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290) found improved individual
evaluations alongside reduced collective novelty.
That result concerns writing, not website design; applying it to interfaces is an inference.
The publisher denied direct access; the research scout inspected its metadata and abstract through Crossref.

Use actual project constraints, personal language, and a recognizable portrait instead of
generic futuristic imagery.
Retain the existing typography and color identity while improving composition and information hierarchy.
Avoid repeated decorative cards, invented dashboards, glowing ornaments, and gratuitous animation.
Review rendered screenshots and real interactions instead of trusting generated code.
